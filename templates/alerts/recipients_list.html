How Notifications Work
  • When a water quality record is encoded and evaluated as UNSAFE or CAUTION, the system automatically creates an alert and dispatches notifications.
  • Recipients with an empty Barangay Filter receive all municipality-wide alerts.
  • Recipients with a specific Barangay Filter only receive alerts for that barangay.
  • In Simulation Mode, messages are only logged — nothing is actually sent. Safe for testing during project defense.
